## Architectural Trade-Offs & Benchmarks

The architectures of Fable 5, Mythos 5, and Kimi K3 differ significantly, leading to distinct trade-offs in their performance. Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are based on the Claude architecture, which is designed for general-purpose natural language processing tasks. Kimi K3, on the other hand, is built on the Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals architecture, which is optimized for long-horizon coding and knowledge work.

| Model | Architecture | Parameters | Context Window | Performance ( FrontierCode Evaluation) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fable 5 | Claude | 2.5T | 1M | 92.5% |
| Mythos 5 | Claude | 2.5T | 1M | 95.1% |
| Kimi K3 | Kimi Delta Attention | 2.8T | 1M | 90.3% |

The performance benchmarks of these models vary across different tasks. Fable 5 and Mythos 5 excel in software engineering tasks, while Kimi K3 demonstrates exceptional performance in long-horizon coding and knowledge work.
