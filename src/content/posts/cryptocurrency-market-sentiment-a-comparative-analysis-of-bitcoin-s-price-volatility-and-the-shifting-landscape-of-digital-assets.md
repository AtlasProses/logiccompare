**Bitcoin's Price Volatility: A Closer Look**

Bitcoin's price has been a subject of interest for many investors and analysts. With its value fluctuating between $60,000 and $70,000 in recent months, it's essential to understand the factors influencing its price. The ongoing Coldcard wallet hack has raised concerns among investors, leading to a slight decrease in Bitcoin's price. However, the overall market sentiment remains bullish, with many experts predicting a long-term increase in value.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Sample data for Bitcoin's price
data = {'Date': ['2026-08-01', '2026-08-02', '2026-08-03'],
        'Price': [62000, 63000, 64000]}

df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# Calculate daily price change
df['Daily Change'] = df['Price'].pct_change()

print(df)
```

**The Shifting Landscape of Digital Assets**

As the cryptocurrency market continues to evolve, we're witnessing a growing trend of executive migration from bitcoin mining to AI and power infrastructure. Matt Prusak, the president and interim CFO of American Bitcoin, has announced his departure to join Giga Energy, a developer and manufacturer of power equipment and AI data center infrastructure. This move underscores the increasing importance of energy infrastructure in supporting both large-scale AI computing and bitcoin mining.

```python
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Sample data for AI and power infrastructure growth
data = {'Year': [2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026],
        'AI Infrastructure Growth': [10, 15, 20, 25, 30],
        'Power Infrastructure Growth': [5, 10, 15, 20, 25]}

df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# Plot the growth of AI and power infrastructure
plt.plot(df['Year'], df['AI Infrastructure Growth'], label='AI Infrastructure Growth')
plt.plot(df['Year'], df['Power Infrastructure Growth'], label='Power Infrastructure Growth')
plt.xlabel('Year')
plt.ylabel('Growth Rate')
plt.title('AI and Power Infrastructure Growth')
plt.legend()
plt.show()
```
