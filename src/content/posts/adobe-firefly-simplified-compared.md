# The Core Engineering Reality & Metric Baselines

Adobe Firefly's GPU-based training infrastructure, built on Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (Amazon EKS), presents a unique set of challenges in terms of observability. With thousands of compute nodes and GPUs, the infrastructure generates high-cardinality telemetry across multiple dimensions, including GPU health and performance metrics, compute and memory metrics, and more.

A critical issue Adobe faced was the high volume of metric data points generated by their training jobs. For instance, a single query window could produce over 1 billion data points, scraped every 30 seconds from 2,000 nodes with 16,000 GPUs. This led to performance issues with their self-hosted Prometheus infrastructure, which struggled to handle queries at such a high cardinality and volume.

To address this challenge, Adobe evaluated Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus as a fully managed alternative that could handle their horizontal scale requirements without the operational overhead of maintaining their own deployment.

## Granular System Breakdown & Architectural Trade-offs

Adobe's observability evolution was an iterative process, with each phase addressing a specific set of limitations and informed by direct feedback from infrastructure users on what mattered most to them.

### Comparison Matrix

| **Metric** | **Self-Managed Prometheus** | **Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus** |

### Architectural Trade-offs

Adobe's decision to adopt Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus was driven by the need for fast query performance at scale, high availability, and scalability. However, this decision also introduced trade-offs, such as the need to curate critical metrics and the potential for lock contention in the memory allocator.

### Gotchas & Risks

When implementing Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus, it's essential to consider the following gotchas and risks:

* Lock contention in the memory allocator can lead to OOM panic traces
* Careful resource allocation is necessary to avoid locking the PostgreSQL WAL disk
* Curating critical metrics is crucial to ensure that infrastructure users have access to the metrics they need to monitor and troubleshoot training jobs independently

By understanding these trade-offs and taking steps to mitigate potential issues, Adobe was able to simplify its observability architecture and improve query performance, infrastructure reliability, and scale.
