The protocol incentivizes liquidity providers to add funds to the liquidity pool by offering a share of the trading fees. However, this mechanism can be vulnerable to market volatility and impermanent loss.

For instance, during the 2020 DeFi summer, Uniswap's liquidity pool experienced significant impermanent loss due to the rapid price appreciation of the underlying assets. This resulted in a substantial decrease in the liquidity pool's value, which in turn affected the protocol's overall liquidity and trading volume.
