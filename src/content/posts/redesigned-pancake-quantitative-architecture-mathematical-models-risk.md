## Institutional DCF Models, Sensitivity Tables & Liquidity Stress Tests

Institutional investors often employ complex quantitative models to evaluate investment opportunities. These models typically involve discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis, sensitivity tables, and liquidity stress tests.

### DCF Model

A basic DCF model can be represented as follows:

DCF = ∑ (CFt / (1 + r)^t)

Where:

* CFt = cash flow at time t
* r = discount rate
* t = time period
